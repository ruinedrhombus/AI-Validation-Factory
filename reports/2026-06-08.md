## 💡 IDEA 1 — PrepPilot
> Plans every client's weekly menu, allergy-checks it, and spits out one combined shopping list and cost sheet — for solo personal chefs and meal-prep operators.

**Who is this for?**
A solo personal chef or one-person meal-prep business cooking weekly for 10–30 regular clients, each with their own diet, allergies, and "please not chicken again" preferences.

**What problem does it solve?**
Every Sunday night the same grind: open a spreadsheet, plan a different week of meals for each client without repeating last week's dishes, double-check nobody's getting the nut/dairy/gluten thing that'll land them in hospital, then reverse-engineer one big grocery list and price it so the week doesn't run at a loss. It's 4–6 hours of error-prone juggling, and one missed allergy is a real safety and reputation disaster.

**Real world example:**
Meet Anjali. She's a personal chef with 18 weekly clients in Bangalore. Every Sunday she spends five hours building menus in a spreadsheet, cross-checking each client's allergy list from memory, then hand-writing a shopping list and tallying costs on a calculator. With PrepPilot she answers a few prompts per client, and in 20 minutes she has 18 personalized menus (none repeating the last 3 weeks), every allergy auto-flagged, a single consolidated shopping list, and a per-client cost breakdown that protects her margin.

**How it works:**
1. Add each client once — diet, allergies, dislikes, portion size, weekly budget.
2. Each week PrepPilot drafts personalized menus that avoid recent repeats and auto-flag any allergy conflict.
3. It rolls everything into one combined shopping list plus a per-client cost sheet — chef reviews, tweaks, sends.

**Price:** $19/mo (up to 15 clients), $39/mo (15+) — one extra client covers it many times over, and it removes a literal safety liability.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — ClientPulse
> Auto-builds each client's weekly marketing report — pulling GA4, Meta, and Google Ads into one branded doc with plain-English commentary in the freelancer's own voice.

**Who is this for?**
A solo marketing freelancer or agency-of-one managing paid/organic for 5–10 small-business clients who each expect a regular performance update.

**What problem does it solve?**
Every Monday the freelancer logs into 3–4 dashboards per client, copies numbers into a template, then writes "here's what happened and what we're doing next" — for every client. It's 4–7 hours of mechanical assembly that produces nothing billable, just the report clients need to feel the retainer is working.

**Real world example:**
Meet Rohan, a freelance performance marketer with 7 clients. Every Monday he loses a full morning pulling GA4, Meta Ads, and Search Console into branded slides and writing the same style of commentary seven times. With ClientPulse he connects each client's accounts once; every Monday a finished, branded report is waiting — numbers pulled, charts built, narrative drafted in his tone — and he just reviews and sends.

**How it works:**
1. Connect each client's GA4 / Meta / Google Ads / Search Console once.
2. ClientPulse pulls the week's data, builds a branded report, and drafts commentary in the freelancer's voice.
3. Freelancer reviews, tweaks the narrative, sends — minutes instead of a morning.

**Price:** $29/mo (up to 5 clients), $49/mo (10) — saves a recurring half-day every single week.
