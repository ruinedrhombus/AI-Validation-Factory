## 💡 IDEA 1 — RoyaltyRoll
> One ledger that pulls every book-sales platform together and tells a self-published author when a platform paid them less than it owed.

**Who is this for?**
The solo self-published author who is "wide" — selling the same books across Amazon KDP, Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play, B&N, IngramSpark, Draft2Digital and audiobook — and earns enough each month that the money actually matters.

**What problem does it solve?**
At the end of every month, royalties land from 6–8 different platforms, in different currencies, on different dates, each with its own confusing report. The author has no single place to see "what did I actually earn," and no way to catch a platform that under-reported sales, paid late, or quietly ate money on currency conversion. So they either build a fragile spreadsheet or just trust the numbers and hope.

**Real world example:**
Meet Priya. She is a wide indie romance author with 14 books. Every month she opens eight dashboards, copies numbers into Google Sheets, fights the dollar-to-rupee conversions, and still can't tell whether Kobo short-changed her. With RoyaltyRoll she connects her accounts once and sees one reconciled ledger — and a flag when expected income doesn't match what hit her bank.

**How it works:**
1. Connect each sales platform once.
2. RoyaltyRoll normalizes everything into one currency and one ledger.
3. It compares expected vs received and flags short, missing, or late payments.

**Price:** $20/month — the author recovers more than that in caught discrepancies and saved monthly admin.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — PostCheck
> Before a creator hits "post" on a paid brand deal, it checks the caption against the brand's contract and flags anything required-but-missing.

**Who is this for?**
The solo social-media creator (roughly 10k–500k followers) who takes paid brand deals and juggles each brand's fiddly list of required tags, hashtags, promo codes and disclosure rules.

**What problem does it solve?**
Every sponsored post comes with contractual fine print — tag @brand, include #ad correctly, use code SAVE20, mention the link, post by Friday. Miss one element and the brand can legitimately withhold or claw back the fee. The creator catches this only by nervously re-reading the contract against their caption every single time.

**Real world example:**
Meet Aditya. He is a 90k-follower fitness creator doing three brand deals a month. Last quarter a brand held his payment because he forgot a required #ad placement. With PostCheck he pastes the contract and his draft caption, and it flags the missing element before he posts — not after the money is gone.

**How it works:**
1. Paste the brand contract/brief and your draft caption.
2. PostCheck extracts every required element (tags, hashtags, code, link, disclosure, deadline).
3. It flags anything missing before you publish.

**Price:** $20/month — cheaper than losing one withheld brand fee.
