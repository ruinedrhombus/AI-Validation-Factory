## 💡 IDEA 1 — MarkGuard
> Checks your t-shirt design against the live US trademark database before you hit publish, so you never get your seller account banned.

**Who is this for?**
A solo print-on-demand seller (Merch by Amazon, Etsy, Redbubble) running their store from a laptop, uploading new designs every week.

**What problem does it solve?**
A POD seller drops a clever phrase onto a shirt — say "Hocus Pocus I Need Coffee to Focus" — not knowing someone trademarked it last month. Amazon's bot catches it after upload, rejects the design, and freezes their account from new uploads for 30+ days. One bad word can cost a month of income, and they live in low-grade fear of it every single listing.

**Real world example:**
Meet Priya. She runs a one-person Merch by Amazon store. Every Sunday she uploads 15 new designs. Twice this year a design got flagged for a trademarked phrase she'd never heard of, and her upload tier got throttled for weeks. With MarkGuard she pastes each phrase, gets a red/yellow/green flag plus the conflicting registered mark in 3 seconds, and swaps the risky ones before publishing.

**How it works:**
1. Paste your design's text (or connect your draft listing).
2. MarkGuard matches every phrase against the live USPTO trademark index and scores the risk.
3. You see the exact conflicting mark and a safe-alternative suggestion before you publish.

**Price:** $19/month — one avoided suspension is worth a month of sales.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — FormFlag
> Your clients send workout videos; AI flags the form mistakes so you review 40 lifts in minutes instead of hours.

**Who is this for?**
A solo online fitness/strength coach with 20–60 remote clients paying $100–200/month, drowning in form-check videos every week.

**What problem does it solve?**
Every week clients upload squat and deadlift videos for feedback. The coach scrubs through each one at 2x speed, types up corrections, and loses an entire evening to it. The more clients they take, the worse the bottleneck — review time, not coaching skill, becomes the cap on their income.

**Real world example:**
Meet Arjun, an online strength coach with 45 clients. Every Monday he faces 40+ form videos and spends 5–6 hours reviewing them. With FormFlag the videos come in pre-analyzed: likely form errors flagged against each exercise's checklist, with draft correction cues he edits in his own voice — turning a 6-hour evening into a 45-minute pass.

**How it works:**
1. Clients upload their lift videos as usual.
2. FormFlag runs computer-vision analysis and flags likely form errors per exercise.
3. The coach gets a triaged list with draft cues, reviews the flagged ones, and sends.

**Price:** $29/month — time saved on review = room for more paying clients.
