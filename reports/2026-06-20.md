## 💡 IDEA 1 — GrantMatch
> Finds the right grants for each of your nonprofit clients and pulls the exact requirements out of every RFP, so you stop reading 40-page PDFs to learn if you even qualify.

**Who is this for?**
A solo freelance grant writer juggling 4–8 small-nonprofit clients, billing $50–150/hour, who spends unpaid hours hunting for opportunities and decoding eligibility rules across federal, state, and foundation sources.

**What problem does it solve?**
A freelance grant writer's income is capped by billable proposal hours, but a big chunk of their week is unpaid: scanning Grants.gov, foundation sites, and newsletters for opportunities that fit each client, then opening every promising RFP to dig out the eligibility, deadline, budget cap, and required attachments before deciding whether to pursue it. Miss a buried "must serve clients in three counties" line and they waste days drafting a proposal that gets tossed on a technicality.

**Real world example:**
Meet Dana. She writes grants for six small nonprofits from her home office. Every Monday she spends three unpaid hours combing funding databases and skimming RFP PDFs to build each client's "go/no-go" shortlist. With GrantMatch she opens one dashboard per client — matched opportunities ranked by fit, each with the eligibility rules, deadline, and required documents already extracted into a checklist — and turns a three-hour hunt into a 20-minute review.

**How it works:**
1. Add each client's profile (mission, location, budget, focus area).
2. GrantMatch matches them to live opportunities from public grant feeds and extracts each RFP's requirements into a structured checklist.
3. You get a ranked, deadline-sorted shortlist per client with go/no-go flags before you spend a billable minute.

**Price:** $29/month — one avoided dead-end proposal pays for a year.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — CakeQuote
> Turns the messy "hi how much for a 2-tier birthday cake?" Instagram DMs into structured quotes, deposits, and a production calendar — so you stop losing orders in your inbox.

**Who is this for?**
A solo home-based custom-cake baker taking 10–25 orders a week through Instagram and WhatsApp DMs, doing all the quoting, scheduling, and deposit-chasing by hand between batches.

**What problem does it solve?**
A home baker's sales channel is their DMs: a flood of half-formed inquiries ("need a cake for Saturday, chocolate, around 20 people") that each require back-and-forth to pin down flavor, size, date, and budget, then a manually typed quote and a deposit request. Orders get buried, double-bookings happen, and the unpaid admin eats the evenings they should be baking.

**Real world example:**
Meet Meera. She bakes custom cakes from home and gets 15–20 Instagram inquiries a week. Each one is a 6-message thread to extract the details, then a quote she types in Notes and a deposit she chases over UPI. With CakeQuote a shareable order form captures flavor, size, date, and inspiration photo up front; she sends a structured quote in two taps, and every confirmed order lands on a production calendar with the deposit tracked.

**How it works:**
1. Share your CakeQuote order-form link in your Instagram bio and DM replies.
2. Customers fill in flavor, size, date, and photos; you get a clean inquiry instead of a thread.
3. Send a quote, collect the deposit, and watch confirmed orders auto-populate your bake calendar.

**Price:** $15/month — one saved double-booking or lost order covers it.
