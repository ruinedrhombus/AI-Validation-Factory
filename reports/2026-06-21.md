## 🔬 TRIAGE — CommissionHound (Run #9, hotbox)

> Expected-vs-received commission reconciliation + overdue-chase for solo
> home-based independent travel advisors.

**Gating kill question (from memory):** What % of target advisors chase
suppliers *directly* vs receive commissions *host-routed*? And are they
already on Tern/TESS? Rule: *if >half host-routed OR Tern-covered, kill.*

**Finding — KILL. Both kill conditions are now true:**

1. **Overwhelmingly host-routed.** Direct supplier credentials are
   "incredibly difficult" for solo advisors — major cruise lines, hotels
   and tour operators require minimum sales volumes or years of experience
   before dealing directly. Most solo advisors therefore book *through* a
   host (70/30→90/10 splits) and receive commissions **host-routed**. The
   minority that can chase suppliers directly are the high-volume,
   credentialed advisors — not the target solo persona.
2. **Tern now ships the exact mechanism.** Tern's *Advisor Commission
   Tracking* explicitly lets you "track overdue payments and log exactly
   what you have received from your host agency or suppliers," plus *Agency
   Commission Reconciliation*. Pricing scales down to solo advisors. TESS
   automates commission tracking + wholesaler payment reconciliation too.

The reconciliation-and-chase loop CommissionHound was built around is now a
native feature of the CRM these advisors already use. **No paid wedge.**

**Verdict: KILL.** (Hotbox 4 → 3.)
