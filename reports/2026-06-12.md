## 💡 IDEA 1 — StaySignal
> Spots which paying members of your community are about to cancel — weeks before they do — and runs the win-back for you.

**Who is this for?**
The solo operator of a paid online community (Skool, Circle, Discord) charging $30–100/month to 100–500 members, whose entire income is monthly renewals.

**What problem does it solve?**
Cancellations arrive as a surprise. A member goes quiet in week 3, stops opening posts in week 5, and cancels in week 7 — and the operator only finds out when the Stripe email lands. By then the member is emotionally gone and unrecoverable. The operator knows the early-warning signs exist in the activity data, but checking 300 members' engagement by hand every week is impossible, so they only ever play defense after the cancel.

**Real world example:**
Meet Marcus. He runs a $49/month Skool community for freelance video editors — 240 members, about $11k MRR. Every month 15–20 members churn, and 70% of them showed the same pattern: stopped posting, stopped logging in, ghosted the weekly call. With StaySignal, every Monday he gets a list of the 12 members drifting toward the exit, each with a one-tap personalized check-in DM ready to send. He saves 4–5 of them a month — that's ~$2,900/year in recovered MRR for ten minutes a week.

**How it works:**
1. Connect your community — StaySignal reads member activity (logins, posts, comments, event attendance) and learns each member's normal rhythm.
2. When a member's engagement drops below their own baseline, they're flagged "at risk" with the reason in plain English ("hasn't posted in 18 days, used to post weekly").
3. StaySignal queues a personalized win-back touch — a check-in DM, a re-onboarding nudge, or an invite to the next live call — for one-tap approval.

**Price:** $35/month — saving two members a month pays for it 3x over, and this persona already pays Skool $99/month.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — PolicyDiff
> Watches every employment-law change in the states your clients operate in, and tells you exactly which client's handbook needs which update — before the client gets fined.

**Who is this for?**
The solo fractional HR consultant serving 5–15 small-business clients, each with employees scattered across multiple US states.

**What problem does it solve?**
Employment law changes constantly — minimum wage, sick-leave rules, pay-transparency requirements, at the state, county, and even city level. The consultant's clients pay her precisely to keep their handbooks legal. So every month she reads law-firm newsletters, SHRM alerts, and state labor sites, then manually cross-references: "Chicago changed paid-leave accrual — which of my 11 clients have Illinois employees, and which handbook sections does that touch?" One missed update is a client penalty with her name on it.

**Real world example:**
Meet Melissa, a fractional HR consultant in Texas with 11 SMB clients whose employees sit in 14 states. Every Tuesday she spends 2–3 hours scanning legal newsletters and mapping changes to clients by hand in a spreadsheet — pure fear-driven work she can't bill cleanly. With PolicyDiff, she enters each client's states and policies once. When Colorado tweaks its leave law, she gets one alert: "Affects clients #3 and #7, handbook sections 4.2 and 6.1, suggested redline attached." Three hours becomes twenty minutes, and the redline becomes a billable client deliverable.

**How it works:**
1. Set up each client once: which states they have employees in, which policies their handbook contains.
2. PolicyDiff monitors federal, state, and local employment-law changes continuously.
3. When a law changes, you get a per-client impact alert — who's affected, which handbook section, and a suggested redline you can review and bill out.

**Price:** $39/month — the incumbent consultant tool (SixFifty) costs $5,000/year; one billable client update per month pays for PolicyDiff ten times over.
