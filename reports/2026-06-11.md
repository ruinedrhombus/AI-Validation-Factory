## 💡 IDEA 1 — MatchLoop
> Runs the entire first-date coordination loop for professional matchmakers — intro, mutual yes, scheduling, reminders, and post-date feedback — without the matchmaker touching it.

**Who is this for?**
The independent matchmaker or dating coach running a solo practice with 10–30 paying clients at $2,000–$10,000 per package, who currently coordinates every date by hand over text and email.

**What problem does it solve?**
Tuesday morning, she has four active matches in flight. Match A said yes Friday but the candidate hasn't replied; Match B needs Thursday rescheduled; Match C went on a date Saturday and she still owes both sides a feedback call. Her morning disappears into a 40-message thread of "does 7pm work for you? let me check with him" — pure relay work between two people who can't be allowed to talk directly yet.

**Real world example:**
Meet Priya, a solo matchmaker with 18 active clients. Every match means ~25 back-and-forth messages: blind intro to both sides, collect two yeses, find a slot both can make, book the restaurant, send reminders, then chase both for post-date feedback within 48 hours. At 5–8 matches in flight, that's 10+ hours a week of pure relay. With MatchLoop she pairs two profiles, hits "launch," and the loop runs itself — she just sees one dashboard: "Match 12: date confirmed Thu 7pm. Match 9: her feedback in, his pending." Ten hours becomes one.

**How it works:**
1. Matchmaker selects two client profiles and clicks "start match" — each side gets a privacy-safe intro card (no contact info) and a yes/no prompt.
2. On double-yes, MatchLoop negotiates a date slot between both parties' availability, confirms venue/time, and sends reminders — staying in the middle so no contact details are exchanged.
3. 24 hours after the date, it collects structured feedback from both sides separately and posts the summary to the dashboard, flagging "second date wanted" or "needs a call."

**Price:** $39/month — her clients pay $2k+ each, so a tool that replaces 10 hours of relay work a week is invisible on the P&L.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — DealGate
> A smart front door for creators' brand-deal inbox — it qualifies every inbound brand inquiry, kills the scams and lowballs automatically, and only puts real money in front of the creator.

**Who is this for?**
The 30k–500k-follower creator or UGC freelancer earning $2k–$15k/month from brand deals, who fields 20–60 inbound "collab?" DMs and emails a week, most of them junk.

**What problem does it solve?**
Tuesday morning: 14 new "we'd love to partner" messages across Instagram DMs, TikTok, and email. Three are scams with phishing links, six offer "free product as payment," two are agencies fishing for rates with no budget — and somewhere in there are one or two real $1,500 deals. Finding them means reading all 14, googling each brand, replying with rates, and tracking who answered. Every week, forever.

**Real world example:**
Meet Aditi, a fitness creator with 120k Instagram followers doing ~$6k/month in deals. Every week she spends 6–8 hours triaging inbound: vetting whether brands are real, answering rate-fishers, dodging "gifted collab" spam, and following up with the two serious ones. With DealGate, her DM auto-reply and email all point to one intake link; brands answer five questions, DealGate verifies the brand is real, auto-declines below her floor with her rate card attached, and pings her only for qualified deals — pre-scored, with a suggested counter. Eight hours becomes forty-five minutes, and lowballs occasionally upgrade themselves to meet her floor.

**How it works:**
1. Creator sets her deal floor, deliverable menu, and usage-rights terms once; gets a branded intake link for DM auto-replies, email signature, and link-in-bio.
2. Every inbound brand goes through structured intake; DealGate verifies the company is real (domain age, site, past creator work), scores the offer against her floor, and auto-handles rejects — polite decline plus rate card.
3. Qualified deals land in a pipeline with score, brand dossier, and suggested counter; she just decides yes/no/negotiate, and DealGate chases brands who go quiet.

**Price:** $25/month — one extra closed deal or one avoided scam pays for a year, and this persona already pays for Beacons, Later, and editing tools.
