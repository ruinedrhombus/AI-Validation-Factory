## 💡 IDEA 1 — ReplayCoach
> Turns a student's game stats into a personalized, written coaching review and drill plan — in the coach's own voice — in minutes instead of hours.

**Who is this for?**
Solo esports coaches (Valorant / League / CS2) with 8–40 paying students, charging $20–60 per session.

**What problem does it solve?**
A solo coach's income is capped by how many students they can review each week. After every student's ranked matches they manually scrub replays, jot down what went wrong, then type a personalized improvement note and pick drills. That's 1–2 hours per student, every week — so taking on more students means working more nights, not earning more. The review work, not the coaching itself, is the ceiling.

**Real world example:**
Meet Aditya. He coaches 18 Valorant players at ₹1,500/session from his bedroom in Pune. Every Sunday he spends his entire day re-watching replays and typing up "you over-rotated on B, your eco-round buys are wrong" notes for each player. He turns away new students because he physically can't review more. With ReplayCoach he connects each student's match account, the tool reads the actual match data — economy, positioning, utility usage, trade timing — and drafts a personalized review plus three targeted drills written in his coaching style. He edits and sends in 10 minutes per student instead of 90.

**How it works:**
1. Student links their game account (or uploads a replay); the tool pulls the structured match data automatically.
2. ReplayCoach drafts a personalized written review + 3 drills, matched to the coach's tone and past notes.
3. Coach tweaks anything off and sends — the roster ceiling disappears.

**Price:** $29/month — one extra student they can now take pays for it many times over.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — LeagueDesk
> Runs the boring back-office of an amateur sports league — fixtures, payments, RSVPs, standings — so the organizer can just show up and play.

**Who is this for?**
Solo organizers of amateur sports communities (Sunday football leagues, padel ladders, run clubs) with 30–150 members who pay match or membership fees.

**What problem does it solve?**
Running a weekend league is a part-time admin job nobody signed up for. Every week the organizer chases RSVPs across WhatsApp, collects ₹200 from 40 people one by one, redoes the fixture spreadsheet when players drop, and manually updates the standings table after results come in. It's hours of unpaid coordination that turns a fun hobby into a chore — and the reason most small leagues quietly die.

**Real world example:**
Meet Sana. She runs a 60-person Saturday football league in Bangalore. Every Thursday she's pinging "you in this week?" to a chaotic WhatsApp group, every Friday she's checking who actually paid, and every Sunday night she's editing a spreadsheet of scores and re-sorting the table. With LeagueDesk, RSVPs and payments collect themselves, fixtures auto-adjust when someone drops, and the standings + a match recap post automatically when scores are entered. She goes from 4 hours of admin a week to almost none.

**How it works:**
1. Organizer sets up the league once — members, fee, schedule.
2. LeagueDesk auto-chases RSVPs, collects payments, and builds the fixtures each week.
3. Scores go in once; standings, payments, and a recap post update themselves.

**Price:** $19/month — cheaper than the hours and the dropped members it saves.
