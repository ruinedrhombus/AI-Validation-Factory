## 💡 IDEA 1 — CommissionHound
> Finds the hotel and tour commissions you were never paid, and chases them for you with the evidence attached.

**Who is this for?**
The solo, home-based independent travel advisor running a one-person leisure travel business under a host agency — 100,000+ of them in the US alone.

**What problem does it solve?**
Hotel commissions structurally leak: bookings route through bedbanks, IATA numbers fall off reservations, and suppliers simply don't pay unless chased. Industry estimates say 40%+ of travel commissions have discrepancies or go unpaid. Advisors today keep a tracking spreadsheet, cross-check stay dates against monthly statements, and write chase emails by hand — often spending more time than the commission is worth, so the small ones get silently written off.

**Real world example:**
Meet Dana, 38, a host-agency advisor in Austin booking ~25 trips a month. Every month the statement lands and three hotel stays that checked out weeks ago aren't on it. She loses most of a day reconciling and chasing, and still writes off the small claims. With CommissionHound she forwards her confirmations as she books; on statement day the tool auto-matches payments, flags $610 unpaid across five stays, and drafts chase emails with confirmation numbers, IATA, and checkout proof attached. She reviews and sends in 20 minutes.

**How it works:**
1. Forward booking confirmations to a dedicated inbox — the tool extracts supplier, guest, stay dates, and expected commission.
2. Forward your monthly host/Onyx/supplier statements — it reconciles expected vs received and flags anything unpaid past checkout + grace period.
3. One click produces an evidence-backed chase packet and tracks each claim until it closes.

**Price:** $29/month — one recovered hotel stay pays for the month, and the leakage is structural, so recovery is recurring.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — CastRadar
> One daily, deduplicated, fit-ranked feed of every public voice-acting casting call scattered across Discord, Twitter/X, Reddit, and casting boards.

**Who is this for?**
The semi-pro freelance voice actor with a home studio, auditioning nights and weekends to go full-time.

**What problem does it solve?**
Voice-acting work is scattered across a dozen Discord servers, Casting Call Club, Reddit casting subs, X hashtags, and indie-game forums. Hunting it means hours of nightly scanning, and deadline-sensitive roles get missed because they were posted in a muted channel. Actors describe the audition hunt as one of the most exhausting parts of the career.

**Real world example:**
Meet Priya, 27, a character VA earning ~$800/month from VO around a day job. She spends 8–10 hours a week scanning sources and still misses fits. With CastRadar she sets her voice profile once; each morning she gets one digest of 12 deduplicated calls ranked by fit, with deadlines and audition specs extracted into a checklist. Scanning drops to 20 minutes a day.

**How it works:**
1. Set your voice profile once: range, accents, languages, paid-only filter, rate floor.
2. CastRadar continuously crawls public casting sources and dedups cross-posted calls into one record.
3. You get a daily digest ranked by profile fit and deadline urgency.

**Price:** $19/month — trivial next to the $495+/year actors already pay a single marketplace.
