## 💡 IDEA 1 — TourYield
> A "set it and forget it" pricing autopilot for independent tour guides — it raises prices on the days that are filling up and quietly discounts the dead ones, so you stop leaving money on the table.

**Who is this for?**
The solo or 2-person tour operator (walking tours, kayak trips, food tours) who sells seats on FareHarbor / Rezdy / Bokun and prices every departure the same flat rate all year.

**What problem does it solve?**
Most small operators set one price and never touch it. A Saturday sunset slot sells out at noon (they could have charged 30% more), while a rainy Tuesday morning runs three people deep at a loss. They know dynamic pricing works but think it's only for airlines and big attractions, so they leave an estimated 8–18% of revenue on the table on the exact same bookings.

**Real world example:**
Meet Priya. She runs heritage walking tours in Jaipur and sells through Rezdy. Every Friday she eyeballs next week's bookings and guesses whether to "run a discount." With TourYield she connects Rezdy once, and from then on each departure auto-adjusts its price by how fast it's filling and how close it is — no spreadsheet, no guessing.

**How it works:**
1. Connect your existing booking system in one click.
2. Pick how aggressive you want pricing (gentle / balanced / max revenue).
3. Each departure's price moves on its own as it fills or empties.

**Price:** $19/month — pays for itself the first weekend a sold-out slot earns 30% more.

## 💡 IDEA 2 — StreamScout
> Tells a Twitch streamer exactly which game to stream tonight to get discovered — the sweet spot where lots of people are watching but few people are streaming.

**Who is this for?**
The growth-stage variety streamer (50–500 followers) who plays different games each week and wants to land in the top rows of a category instead of being buried under 800 other channels.

**What problem does it solve?**
Small streamers guess at what to play. Pick a megagame and you're invisible; pick a dead one and nobody's searching. The winning move — high viewer demand, low streamer competition — is a moving target that changes daily, and they have no easy way to see it.

**Real world example:**
Meet Diego. 230 followers, streams after his day job. Tonight he flips a coin between the new AAA release (10,000 streamers) and an indie game (40 viewers). With StreamScout he opens the app, sees tonight's three best-opportunity categories scored for his channel size, and picks one in 30 seconds.

**How it works:**
1. Tell it your channel size and the genres you'll play.
2. It scores every category on demand-vs-competition for tonight.
3. You pick from the top three and go live.

**Price:** $15/month — one good category pick can 5× your concurrent viewers.
