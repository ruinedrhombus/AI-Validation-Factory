### Persona types that resonate with Shivam:
- Solo SaaS founders with recurring operational pain
- Prosumers in lifestyle spaces (fitness, dating, travel, food)
- NOT enterprise buyers, NOT pure consumers

### Idea categories that keep getting killed:
- Episodic pain tools (only needed once every few months)
- Vitamin tools (nice to have, easy to postpone buying)
- Generic AI wrappers solvable with ChatGPT prompt

### Mechanisms that generate discussion:
- Weekly recurring pain with clear before/after
- Moat based on integration or data (not just prose generation)
- Clear market gap between free tools and expensive enterprise
